Unarmed combat fighter — provenance and permissions

REFERENCE / CONTACT GUIDE MEDIA
The existing original earth-brown commoner from wuxia-duel-foes is the sole
character identity. Its original reference and all old sprite files are preserved.
Two selected exact contact-pose guides (body punch and low open palm) were made
with the built-in imagegen service. A first higher palm guide was edited once to
lower the hand; that initial higher guide is not selected. Exact prompts/source
hashes and uniform alignment are in combat-motion-guides/ evidence.

ANIMATED MEDIA
Eight local LTX-2.5 Q8 takes were generated: six initial takes and two guided
attack retries. Seven takes contribute final frames. The initial high punch is
excluded entirely; the initial non-striking palm contributes only defensive
redirection. The initial forward part of the retreat take is excluded. Existing
walk and hurt frames are reused for walk, flinch and kneeling defeat.

CODE
Inference used the existing dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx 0.18.0 runtime (MIT code), recorded
runtime revision 747b30ef10e92a011e85b2aa0f6c4ee86ced5218. Runtime/weights are not
bundled. The packer and postproduction scripts were authored for this workspace.
Code provenance is separate from generated-media permissions.

POSTPRODUCTION
FFmpeg chroma key/despill, original-reference palette, reviewed actual source
poses, uniform half-scale packing, root stabilization on the backward segment,
authored preparation/contact/recovery and editorial support-loop closure.
No limb deformation, fabricated reversed forward gait, or generated audio is
integrated. PNGs use straight alpha; preview GIF/JPEG backgrounds are opaque.
